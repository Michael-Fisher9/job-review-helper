# Privacy Policy for Job Review Helper

## Information Collection and Use

The Job Review Helper extension is designed with privacy in mind and collects minimal data:

- **Company Names**: The extension may detect company names from the web pages you visit when you activate the extension. This information is used solely to search for company reviews and is not stored or transmitted except to the review websites (Glassdoor or Indeed).

- **User Preferences**: Your preferences (such as preferred review source and auto-detection settings) are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API and are not transmitted to any external servers.

## Data Storage

All preferences are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. No user data is sent to our servers or third parties except for the search queries sent directly to Glassdoor or Indeed when you request company reviews.

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

- `activeTab`: To access the current tab to detect company names
- `scripting`: To inject scripts to interact with web pages
- `storage`: To save your preferences locally
- `tabs`: To manage browser tabs and windows

## Third-Party Services

When you use the extension to view company reviews, your search query (company name) is sent to either Glassdoor or Indeed, depending on your selected preference. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for information on how they handle your data:

- [Glassdoor Privacy Policy](https://www.glassdoor.com/about/privacy.htm)
- [Indeed Privacy Policy](https://www.indeed.com/legal?id=privacy)

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
