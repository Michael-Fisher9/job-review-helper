### Job Review Helper

Streamline your job search by instantly accessing company reviews without leaving the job posting page!

Job Review Helper is a lightweight Chrome extension that allows you to quickly view Glassdoor or Indeed company reviews while browsing job postings on sites like Handshake, LinkedIn, Indeed, and more.

#### Key Features:
• **Smart Company Detection**: Automatically identifies the company name on job posting pages
• **Highlight to Search**: Simply highlight any text on the page to use it as your company search
• **Choice of Review Sources**: Toggle between Glassdoor and Indeed reviews
• **Convenient Popup Window**: View reviews in a resizable, draggable popup that stays on top
• **Minimal Permissions**: Only accesses the current tab when you click the extension button

#### How It Works:
1. Navigate to any job posting page
2. Click the Job Review Helper icon in your toolbar
3. The extension will detect the company name or use your highlighted text
4. A popup window will appear with reviews from your preferred source

#### Perfect For:
• Job seekers researching potential employers
• Students browsing university job boards like Handshake
• Professionals evaluating companies on LinkedIn
• Anyone who wants to save time during their job search

#### Privacy:
Job Review Helper respects your privacy and only accesses the current tab when you activate it. No data is collected or stored beyond your preferences (review source choice and auto-detection setting).
